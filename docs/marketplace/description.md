**Collect reviews and star ratings on your own site: a review form for any entry, moderation in the Control Panel, and averages, reviews and review schema in your templates.**

Other review addons copy reviews in from Google, Yotpo or Trustpilot. Reviews collects them on your site, from your visitors and customers, and keeps them in flat files or your database.

## Review anything

Products, books, courses, recipes, services: any entry, in the collections you choose. An overall rating out of five, optional ratings for criteria you set (Quality, Value), a title and the review.

## Guests or signed-in users

Guests give a name and email (never shown). Signed-in reviewers can change or delete their review.

## Moderation

**Content → Reviews**: a queue of pending reviews, approve, reject, spam, bulk actions, notes, and a **public reply** under any review. Permissions for each, a dashboard widget, and a field showing an entry's rating.

## Fair by design

No setting publishes or hides reviews by their stars, and editors can't change what reviewers wrote, in line with UK, EU and US rules on fake and suppressed reviews.

## Averages and breakdowns

The average, the count, how many of each star, and each criterion's average, cached until a review changes.

## Sort, filter and paginate

Newest, highest, lowest or most helpful; by rating, verified purchases or photos. "Was this helpful?" votes.

## Ready-made, if you want it

Two tags give you a finished reviews section: the average and star breakdown, the reviews with sorting, pages and "helpful" buttons, and the form. Its styles take your site's own fonts and colours and follow its dark mode, and every colour, size and gap is a CSS variable you can change. Or publish the views and stylesheet and make them yours.

## Review schema

JSON-LD with the average rating and latest reviews, for star ratings in search results: Product, Book, Course, Recipe, or any schema.org type.

## Works with SEO Pro

Already printing schema with an SEO addon? Add the rating and reviews to its own JSON-LD instead of a second block, so search engines see one product. Step-by-step for SEO Pro, and the same approach for others.

## Spam protection without puzzles

A honeypot, a time trap and a link limit on every form, rate limits, and optionally a captcha: Cloudflare Turnstile, hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA v2 or v3, or your own.

## Photos

If you want them: re-saved without location data, and kept private until the review is approved.

## Emails

New reviews to editors; approvals and replies to reviewers.

## Cargo shops

With [Cargo](https://builtwithcargo.dev): verified purchase badges, reviews from buyers only, and emails asking customers to review what they bought, a set time after their order.

## Private by default

No IP addresses kept. Export or forget someone's reviews with one command.

## Or your own markup

Every part is a tag, for markup of your own. Works without JavaScript, and on statically cached pages. Antlers or Blade: every example has both.

## Requirements

- Statamic 6.35 or later
- PHP 8.3 or later
- **Statamic Pro** for signed-in reviewers and Control Panel permissions. Guests can review without it.
- Optional: Cargo 1.15 or later, for verified purchases
